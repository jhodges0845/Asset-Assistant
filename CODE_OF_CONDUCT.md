# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Asset Assistant welcomes artists, developers, testers, documentarians, and newcomers. We want participation to be respectful, constructive, and accessible.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, including when discussing technical or artistic disagreements.
- Give actionable, specific feedback; critique work rather than people.
- Respect contributors' time, experience, and creative ownership.
- Be considerate of different backgrounds and skill levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning comments, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information without consent, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For community-conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer privately through an available GitHub contact channel. Do not post sensitive allegations or personal information in public issues. If no private channel is available, request one publicly without sharing sensitive details.

Maintainers may remove content, request changes in behavior, limit participation, or block accounts as appropriate. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in all circumstances.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces.
